How to Search Court Cases by CNR Number

A CNR (Case Number Record) is a unique identifier for an Indian court case. Use it on Court Case History to open case status, history, orders and daily business.

Open CNR Search Browse courts

Steps

  1. Open CNR Search.
  2. Enter the full CNR without spaces (typically 16 characters).
  3. Submit to load case details, petitioner/respondent, hearings, orders and daily business when available.

Tips

  • Double-check digits and letters — a single wrong character returns no match.
  • If you do not have a CNR, use Case Status Search with court, case type, number and year.
  • After results load, export Orders, Case History or Daily Business as PDF.
